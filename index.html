Rui Zhou 周瑞

I am a master's student in Electronic Information at the University of Science and Technology of China, where I am a member of MIRACLE Lab, advised by Prof. Shaohua Kevin Zhou.

My research focuses on how generative models respond to their conditions: how to update one sound without changing the others, and how to teach image generators to follow different spatial instructions. I also work on efficient self-supervised learning for medical images.

Image Generation · Post-training2026 — Present

Teaching Image Generators to Follow Spatial Instructions

Question. If the teacher provides correct images, why might a student still struggle to switch between left/right or above/below instructions for the same objects?

Approach. Pair teacher images with opposing spatial instructions, encode them with the student model's VAE, and train from a shared noisy latent toward the condition-specific targets. The method is adapted to UNet noise prediction and MMDiT Flow Matching; only student LoRA parameters are updated.

55.69%Left/right four-instruction success · paired, 64K
55.22%Above/below four-instruction success · paired, 64K
36.53%Left/right success · ordinary SFT, 192K

With the same 9,216 teacher images, paired training at 64K updates outperformed ordinary SFT at 192K updates on both evaluated spatial relations. An L2-regularized setting reached 60.59% on left/right instructions and 59.23% on full GenEval. Color/counting evaluations are also included to measure changes beyond the spatial task.

Research in progress. The results describe the reported evaluation settings, not a claim of universally improved image generation.

Video-to-Audio · Inference Control2026

LocalSoundBench & Regional Flow

Question. When one visual event changes in a short video, can an audio generator replace only the corresponding sound and keep the rest intact?

Approach. Use paired visual edits and controlled noise/temporal experiments to distinguish event recognition from temporal placement errors. Regional Flow compares full-video and event-conditioned velocity fields, then applies the difference inside the relevant audio interval without re-training the base generator.

2,048Paired visual edits in LocalSoundBench
5.4 → 44.5%Joint edit-and-preservation success · MMAudio

We also used fixed-noise, event-order, and intermediate-feature tests to study why a generator can identify a sound yet place it in the wrong interval.

Causal Analysis of Register Tokens in Qwen-Image

Analyzing how register tokens contribute to information routing and content in joint-attention image diffusion models. The current experimental framework includes controlled donor substitutions, token controls, layer sweeps, and sampling-trajectory analysis.

A separate exploratory research direction; detailed results remain outside the publication list.
